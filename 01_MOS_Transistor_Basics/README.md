Lecture 01 – MOS Transistor Basics-I

1. Introduction

A MOSFET (Metal-Oxide-Semiconductor Field-Effect Transistor) is a semiconductor device controlled by an electric field.

2. MOSFET Terminals

A MOS transistor has four terminals:

- Gate (G)
- Source (S)
- Drain (D)
- Body or Bulk (B)

3. Types of MOS Transistors

- NMOS: Uses electrons as the majority charge carriers.
- PMOS: Uses holes as the majority charge carriers.

4. Important Concepts

- Gate voltage controls the transistor's operation.
- MOSFETs are important building blocks of digital integrated circuits.
- NMOS and PMOS transistors are used together in CMOS technology.
